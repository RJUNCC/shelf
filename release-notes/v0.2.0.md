Learning workspaces, answers you can see, and animated lessons with a voice.

- **Workspaces**: add a course's slides (PDF or PowerPoint) beside your books and sort them into folders. Each deck has an outline of slide titles, thumbnails, search, your own notes per slide, and a history of the explanations you've watched. Shelf suggests the book sections each slide comes from; link them with a click and open them in the book.
- **Practice tests and study plans**: be tested on a slide, a deck, a folder or everything; paste your teacher's exam review and get a day-by-day plan with the slides for each topic.
- **Visual answers**: formulas are typeset (chemistry too), processes become diagrams, numbers become charts, and definitions, examples and common mistakes get their own boxes, in Ask AI, practice tests and flashcards.
- **Animated lessons**: *Explain this slide*, or *Explain visually* on a passage or practice question, writes a short lesson that's written out by hand on a whiteboard, chalkboard or notebook page while it's explained, with diagrams drawn as they're described, captions and follow-up questions.
- **Voice-overs**: pick a voice under *Local AI* (Kokoro's 28 voices, Kokoro HD, or the lighter Lessac), try it, and download it once. It runs on your computer, with a volume slider in the lesson. The two Piper voices of 0.1 became Lessac.
- **The library in a panel**: your books, workspaces and study tools also sit in a panel beside every tab, which folds away with Ctrl+B. The Library tab stays for a big view.
