The first release of Shelf: a library and reader for EPUB and PDF books.

- **Library**: covers, collections, reading progress, search, multi-select.
- **Reading**: tabs, numbered outlines (built for scanned PDFs too), highlights and notes with undo, Kindle-like themes, cleaned-up scans.
- **Studying**: flashcards with spaced repetition, practice tests written from a chapter (with hands-on code, Markov chain, molecule and formula questions), Ask this book with page citations, search across every book, and a knowledge graph of your notes.
- **Local AI** with Ollama, optional, on your own computer.
- **Updates** from inside Shelf, checked against SHA-256 checksums.
